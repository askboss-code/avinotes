Input mode

Active stylus mode: only browser events identified as a pen can write. If your rechargeable stylus is passive/capacitive and acts like a finger, switch to Universal / capacitive mode.
Palm-rejection note: on a passive capacitive stylus, iPad Safari may report both stylus and finger/palm as touch. Contact-size filtering can reduce broad touches but cannot guarantee perfect palm rejection. For best results use a stylus that supports iPad palm rejection or an active stylus recognized as a pen.
